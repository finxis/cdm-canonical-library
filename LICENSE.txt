3. CONTENT CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Files in this Library are classified as one of:

  GENERIC EXAMPLE   — a reference representation illustrating how a product is
                      structured in CDM. Provided for reference and education.

  REAL-WORLD        — a validated CDM representation of an actual, identified
                      tradeable contract, current as of the stated as-of date.
                      Real-world content is maintained reference data and its
                      accuracy is time-sensitive.

  PROVISIONAL       — content that relies on proposed CDM model extensions that
                      are not yet accepted into the FINOS CDM standard, and/or
                      has not been endorsed by the CDM working group (CRWG).
                      Provisional content represents Finxis's valid-CDM
                      interpretation and is subject to change.

Individual files indicate their classification via their sidecar metadata where
applicable.
